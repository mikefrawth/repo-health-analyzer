What does the Health Score actually do?

Verdict: this prototype found two real defects, both now fixed in backend/app/scoring.py and metrics.py (see docs/adr/ for the record). (1) Commit activity counted every commit in the clone with no regard for age, so a repository dead for three years got full marks for having a past — fixed by counting only commits inside a 90-day window. (2) Renormalisation let an unmeasurable repository (no manifest, no complexity analyser) reach a perfect 100 — fixed by capping the score in proportion to how much was actually measured. This page still runs the original, pre-fix formula, kept as the primary source for how the defects were found — the module below is intentionally not updated to match.
